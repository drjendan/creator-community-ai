# UpNexx product overview

UpNexx is a multi-tenant SaaS product owned and operated by Nexx Jenn Technologies. It gives podcasters, coaches, authors, speakers, educators, ministries, consultants, and thought leaders one branded place to publish, teach, build community, sell memberships, host events, and provide AI-assisted experiences.

The product has five surfaces in one Next.js deployment:

1. The public UpNexx sales site (`/`)
2. Tenant-branded public sites (`/demo/[tenant-slug]` during local development)
3. Platform super-administration (`/platform-admin`)
4. Tenant administration (`/dashboard`)
5. Tenant member experiences (`/demo/[tenant-slug]/member`)

The current tenant is the fictional `AI at Work` example. Product and tenant data are mock data until Supabase is configured. Mock behavior is labeled in the UI.

## Commercial model

The public site presents Creator, Growth, Professional, and Enterprise plans plus a Founding Creator onboarding offer. Payment processor charges, third-party AI usage, domains, and professional services remain separate.
